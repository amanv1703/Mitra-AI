# MITRA AI — Buildathon Pitch Deck & Value Proposition

## 🎯 Tagline
> **"From business data to evidence-backed action."**  
> *Don't just see what happened. Know why — and decide what to do next.*

---

## 1. The Core Problem
Modern e-commerce and retail merchants are drowning in raw data. They have dashboards for Shopify, Razorpay, Google Analytics, Warehouse WMS, and Delhivery logistics.

Yet when revenue drops:
1. **Siloed Telemetry**: The merchant sees revenue is down, but doesn't know if it's due to payment gateway timeouts, stockout shortfall gaps, or carrier delivery delays.
2. **Analysis Paralysis**: Calculating lead-time shortfall risk, revenue at risk, and demand surges requires manual spreadsheets.
3. **Dangerous Automation**: Pure LLM agents hallucinate or blindly trigger destructive actions without guardrails or verification.

---

## 2. The Solution: MITRA AI
MITRA is an **AI Business Operator** for merchants that transforms passive dashboards into an active, governed intelligence and execution layer.

The core loop:
$$\text{DETECT} \longrightarrow \text{INVESTIGATE} \longrightarrow \text{DIAGNOSE} \longrightarrow \text{SIMULATE} \longrightarrow \text{PROPOSE} \longrightarrow \text{HUMAN APPROVAL} \longrightarrow \text{EXECUTE} \longrightarrow \text{VERIFY} \longrightarrow \text{AUDIT}$$

---

## 3. Key Differentiators

| Traditional Dashboards | Generic Chatbots | MITRA AI Business Operator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shows raw numbers (What happened) | Generic text advice without real SQL data | Deterministic cross-domain root-cause attribution |
| Passive observation only | Hallucinates metrics; zero verification | Quantified 3-tier revenue at risk (Confirmed, Estimated, Potential) |
| Manual operator spreadsheets | No execution capabilities | 100% Read-only What-If Counterfactual Simulations |
| No safety boundaries | Uncontrolled API actions | Centralized Policy Engine, 2-Step Confirmation, 0.0% Unauthorized Action Rate |
